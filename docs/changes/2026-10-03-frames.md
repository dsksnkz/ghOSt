## Changes

- Rail and calendar outer corners are 7 px; the staged sidebar uses the same outer radius. Nested card radius follows `max(0, outerRadius - padding)` where applicable.
- The rail uses a three-position workspace wheel, grouped monochrome SVG controls, and a 46 px frame. Hover text is disabled while accessible names remain.
- The calendar frame is 733 × 308 px on the 1920 × 1080 display, preserving the 733 × 310 reference ratio. Weather is on the left, GPU/RAM/CPU liquid diamonds are centered, and the date grid is on the right. Typography was increased after reviewing the native crop. London weather comes from the user's chosen location; no weather values are invented on the live desktop.
- The frame drops into place; its six content groups reveal in a fresh random order over roughly 1.5 seconds. Rain and storm effects follow actual condition codes. Reduced motion removes the staged reveal and moving effects. Real usage determines liquid area, and sampling stops when the calendar closes.
- The staged sidebar contains network and Bluetooth lists, volume, brightness status and notifications status. Its reusable body also renders in an isolated sample-data preview without publishing nearby SSIDs.
- The rail power control opens a compact menu. Sleep, log out, restart and shut down require a second confirmation click. Escape/cancel exits without an action. Lock reports the missing independent configuration rather than calling another rice. The installer no longer rewrites another rice's shell.
